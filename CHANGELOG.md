# RollOS Official Product Changelog & Release Notes (2026)

All notable product updates, dispatch enhancements, and booking engine improvements for [**RollOS Dumpster Rental Software**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/) (serving small and mid-size roll-off waste haulers from **1 to 25+ trucks**) are documented in this file.

---

## [v2.4.0] — October 2026: Multi-Truck Growth Fleet & Contractor Net-30 Workflows
### Added
- **1 to 25+ Truck Visual Dispatch Scaling:** Expanded the [**Visual Drag-and-Drop Dispatch Board**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/features/dispatch-board) for mid-size regional C&D and residential roll-off fleets managing 50 to 350+ serialized containers across multiple yard locations.
- **Commercial Contractor Net-30 & Offline Payment Logging ($29/mo Starter & $149/mo Growth):** Dispatch manual phone orders and repeat roofing/construction contractors with $0-fee cash, check, and invoice payment tracking. See [**RollOS Pricing**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/pricing).
- **1-Click "Absorb Fee" Checkout Toggle:** Haulers on the $0/month Free Plan can now toggle whether the flat $12 online reservation lock fee is paid by the renter at checkout or absorbed into the base container price.

---

## [v2.3.0] — September 2026: Automated Scale Ticket OCR & Tonnage Overage Billing
### Added
- **Measured Tonnage & Landfill Scale Ticket Billing:** Drivers upload transfer station weight slips directly via their [**1-Tap SMS Magic Link**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/features/driver-routes), and RollOS automatically calculates included tonnage vs. overage tons and bills the customer's Stripe card on file. Learn more at [**Automated Tonnage Billing**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/features/tonnage-billing).
- **Free Interactive Hauler Calculators:** Launched the public [**Dumpster Tonnage & Overage Calculator**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/tools/tonnage-calculator) and [**Software Savings ROI Calculator**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/tools/savings-calculator).

---

## [v2.2.0] — August 2026: Zero-App Driver SMS Magic Links & Digital Damage Waivers
### Added
- **Zero-App Driver SMS Routing:** Eliminated mandatory iOS/Android App Store downloads. Dispatchers send expiring SMS route links with 1-tap Google Maps & Apple Maps navigation and timestamped driveway drop-off photo proof.
- **Mandatory Digital Driveway Liability Waivers:** Integrated e-signature rental agreements covering driveway cracks, overhead wires, prohibited hazardous waste, and fill-line limits prior to checkout completion. Download the free [**Dumpster Rental Contract Template**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/templates/dumpster-rental-contract).
