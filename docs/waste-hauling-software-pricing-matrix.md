## Zero-Downtime Migration Checklist for Existing Haulers

Migrating an active 1 to 25+ truck roll-off operation from legacy software or spreadsheets into [**RollOS**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/) takes under 15 minutes with zero website downtime:

1. **Configure Container Buckets & Base Pricing (2 Minutes):** Enter your active container sizes (`10yd`, `15yd`, `20yd`, `30yd`, `40yd`),yard counts, included rental days, included tonnage allowances, and per-ton overage rates.
2. **Connect Stripe Express Payouts (1 Minute):** Link your business checking account so upfront online bookings and post-pickup tonnage overages deposit straight to your bank.
3. **Deploy Your Booking Link or 1-Line Embed (1 Minute):** Paste the 1-line `<script>` embed onto your existing website or place your hosted booking URL (`rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/book/yourcompany`) onto your Google Business Profile and Facebook page.
4. **Import Active On-Site Cans to the Dispatch Board:** Log any containers currently deployed on customer driveways or job sites onto the [**Visual Dispatch Board**](https://rolloffdumpstersoftware.com/features/dispatch-board) so pickup dates and scale ticket billing continue seamlessly.
